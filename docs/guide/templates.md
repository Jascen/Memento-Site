# Character Templates
!!! tip "Don't worry!"
	Memento consciously targets a Casual audience, but we want things to require a bit of effort. Generally speaking, you should be able to experience the full game using the default server settings as long as your character has a way to deal damage and heal.

## Basic Templates

---

### Elementalist
- Play style
	- Elemental pet summoner into ranged caster
- Required Skills
	- [Elementalism](skills-primary.md#elementalism)
- Suggested Skills
	- [Focus](skills-primary.md#focus)
	- [Healing](skills-primary.md#healing)
	- [Meditation](skills-primary.md#meditation)
	- [Spiritualism](skills-primary.md#spiritualism)
- Warnings
	- Stamina is a Reagent. Be sure to keep Refresh potions with you

### Knight
- Play style
	- Well rounded melee damage build
- Required Skills
	- Weapon Specialization ([Bludgeoning](skills-primary.md#bludgeoning) or [Fencing](skills-primary.md#fencing) or [Fist Fighting](skills-primary.md#fist-fighting) or [Swordsmanship](skills-primary.md#swordsmanship))
	- [Focus](skills-primary.md#focus)
	- [Knightship](skills-primary.md#knightship)
	- [Parrying](skills-primary.md#parrying)
- Suggested Skills
	- [Anatomy](skills-primary.md#anatomy)
	- [Healing](skills-primary.md#healing)
	- [Magic Resistance](skills-primary.md#magic-resistance)
	- [Tactics](skills-primary.md#tactics)
- Warnings
	- Taking damage and casting Knightship spells consume Stamina. Be sure to keep Refresh potions with you

### Mage
- Play style
	- Ranged caster
- Required Skills
	- [Magery](skills-primary.md#magery)
	- [Psychology](skills-primary.md#psychology)
- Suggested Skills
	- [Inscription](skills-secondary.md#inscription)
	- [Meditation](skills-primary.md#meditation)
	- [Mercantile](skills-primary.md#mercantile)

### Marksman
- Play style
	- Ranged damage build
- Required Skills
	- [Marksmanship](skills-primary.md#marksmanship)
- Suggested Skills
	- If bows/crossbows: [Bowcraft](skills-secondary.md.md#bowcrafting) + [Lumberjacking](skills-secondary.md.md#lumberjacking)
	- If throwing gloves: [Mining](skills-secondary.md.md#mining) + [Tinkering](skills-secondary.md.md#Tinkering)
	- [Anatomy](skills-primary.md#anatomy)
	- [Bushido](skills-primary.md#bushido)
	- [Focus](skills-primary.md#focus)
	- [Healing](skills-primary.md#healing)
	- [Knightship](skills-primary.md#knightship)
	- [Tactics](skills-primary.md#tactics)
- Warnings
	- Taking damage and casting Knightship spells consume Stamina. Be sure to keep Refresh potions with you

### Tamer
- Play style
	- ~~Macro~~ *Grind* skills until your fingers bleed and then grind more
	- After finding the right (broken) pets, steamroll the rest of the game
	- Eventually just loot while pets kill everything
- Required Skills
	- [Druidism](skills-primary.md#druidism)
	- [Herding](skills-primary.md#herding)
	- [Taming](skills-primary.md#taming)
	- [Veterinary](skills-primary.md#veterinary)
- Suggested Skills
	- [Magery](skills-primary.md#magery)

## Advanced Templates

---

### Death Knight
- Where do I start?
	- A lore book (it gets saved to your Library)
- Reagents
	- Kill humanoids and steal souls
- Required Skills
	- [Knightship](skills-primary.md#knightship)

### Holy Man AKA Priest
- Where do I start?
	- Find a *mallet and stake* (this is a rare drop from anything in the world)
	- Hunt Vampires and speak with a Priest
- Reagents
	- Kill undead and supernatural creatures to prove your worth
- Required Skills
	- [Healing](skills-primary.md#healing)
	- [Spiritualism](skills-primary.md#spiritualism)

### Jester
- Where do I start?
	- Talk to a Jester and purchase a *Bag of Tricks*
- Reagents
	- Tithe to Prank Bag at Jester
- Required Skills
	- [Begging](skills-primary.md#begging)
	- [Psychology](skills-primary.md#psychology)

### Jedi / Syth
- Where do I start?
	- A lore book (it gets saved to your Library)
- Reagents
	- Kill demonic creatures
- Skills
	- [Psychology](skills-primary.md#psychology)
	- [Swordsmanship](skills-primary.md#swordsmanship)
	- [Tactics](skills-primary.md#tactics)

### Mystic AKA Monk
- Where do I start?
	- Find a Dojo, speak to a Monk, and purchase a *Monk's Tome*
- Reagents
	- Tithe at shrines/ankhs
- Skills
	- [Fist Fighting](skills-primary.md#fist-fighting)
	- [Focus](skills-primary.md#focus)
	- [Meditation](skills-primary.md#meditation)

### Shinobi
- Where do I start?
	- Find a Dojo and purchase a *Shinobi Scroll* from a Monk
- Reagents
	- Tithe at shrines/ankhs
- Required Skills
	- [Ninjitsu](skills-primary.md#ninjitsu)
