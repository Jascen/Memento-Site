# Secondary Skills
In an effort to make resource gathering and crafting more accessible, Memento has created *Secondary Skills*. The only exception to this rule is **Cartography** and **Seafaring**.

Each of the following skills <u>will not be counted</u> against your character's total skill cap

## Crafting
### Alchemy
- Role
	- Craft powerful potions to augment your gameplay
	- Brew magical Elixirs to enhance skills
	- Summon slimy companions to aid you in combat
	- Soak the battlefield with dangerous liquids
- Tools
	- Mortar and pestle
- Related
	- [Tinkering](#tinkering) - craft your own tools

### Blacksmithing
- Role
	- Craft metal armor and weapons
	- Layer tough scales to create magical armors
	- Refine rare metals into powerful armor and weapons
- Tools
	- Scaling tools
	- Smith's hammer
- Related
	- [Mining](#mining) - gather ore and smelt into ingots for crafting
	- [Tinkering](#tinkering) - craft your own tools

## Gathering
### Forensics (gathering)
- Role
	- Dig up graves to uncover treasure and bones
	- Carve up creatures for meat, leather, skins, and precious gem blocks
- Tools
	- Grave shovel
	- Skinning knife
- Related
	- [Blacksmithing](#blacksmithing) - generate more gem blocks from elementals
	- Forensic Evaluation - old skill name
	- [Tailoring](#tailoring) - generate more skins from creatures
	- [Tinkering](#tinkering) - craft your own tools

### Lumberjacking
- Role
	- Harvest logs from normal and rich trees
- Tools
	- Hatchet (or any axe)
- Related
	- [Tinkering](#tinkering) - craft your own tools

### Mining
- Role
	- Gather ore from cave floors, mountainsides, and glistening ore veins
	- Refine ore and precious metals into craftable ingots
	- Dig up precious gems
- Tools
	- Pickaxe
	- Shovel
- Related
	- [Tinkering](#tinkering) - craft your own tools
