# Primary Skills
Most skills are considered *Primary* skills. The following skills <u>will be counted</u> against your character's total skill cap.

## Miscellaneous

### Arms Lore
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Craft*{ .chip .chip-note }
*Gold*{ .chip .chip-question }
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Adds weapon damage increase attribute to crafted items
- Adds bonus resistances to crafted items
- Use to identify magical weapons & equipment
	- The more skilled you are, the more likely you will find them more
- Reduce rate of durability loss during combat
- Related
	- [Mercantile](#mercantile) - another skill used to identify items
	- [Tasting](#tasting) - another skill used to identify items

### Begging
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Gold*{ .chip .chip-question }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! warning "Your Fame and Karma may be negatively impacted each time you successfully use this skill"

- Used to determine success rate of *Jester* Pranks
- Make vendors take pity on you; they charge lower fees and give you more gold when you sell your items.
	- Adjust your demeanor by using the skill on yourself
- Use the skill on an NPC to govel before them in hopes of getting some spare gold
- Use the skill on hostile creatures to plead with them to leave you alone
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - disengage from combat
	- [Peacemaking](#peacemaking) - pacifies creatures but uses music
	- [Psychology](#psychology) - *Jester* class pre-requisite

### Camping
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }
*Pet*{ .chip .chip-bug }

- Use a bladed item on a tree to cut kindling from from it
- Use kindling to start a small campfire
- Campfires provides out-of-combat hitpoint and stamina regeneration to the well fed
- A bedroll and campfire together double recovery, but only for the owner of the bedroll
- Pitch a tent for access to a secure resting area. Master campers can even do this in dungeons!
- Grandmasters may purchase a hitching post to stable pets from your home
- Skilled campers also have a greater resilience to hunger and thirst
- Related
	- [Cooking](skills-secondary.md#cooking) - cook food at a campfire
	- [Taming](#taming) - grandmasters can stable pets at home with a hitching post

### Cartography
*Craft*{ .chip .chip-note }
*Gold*{ .chip .chip-question }
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Map the many lands you explore
- Decode treasure maps
- Maps help you navigate, and sea charts make travel on the high seas easier
- A boat's tillerman is skilled in navigation. Try adding waypoints to one of your maps and give it to him
- Tools
	- Map maker's pen
	- Blank scrolls
	- Shovel (to dig up treasure)
- Related
	- [Lockpicking](#lockpicking) - unlock treasure chests
	- [Lumberjacking](skills-secondary.md#lumberjacking) - gather bark fragments used for blank scrolls
	- [Mining](skills-secondary.md#mining) - reduce how close you have to be to dig up treasure
	- [Remove Trap](#remove-trap) - remove deadly traps from chests
	- [Seafaring](#seafaring) - reduce time required to reach a treasure chest

### Mercantile
!!! info "Classic name: Item Identification"

*Gold*{ .chip .chip-question }
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Persuade town vendors to pay up to double when you sell them items
- Players who are well skilled are also more likely to convince vendors to loosen their purse strings a bit more than normal
- Use on unidentified clothing, jewelry, and scrolls to identify them
	- Can also be used on artwork and relics to determine their worth
	- Skilled merchants are more likely to find these types of items
- Related
	- [Arms Lore](#arms-lore) - another skill used to identify items
	- [Begging](#begging) - another skill that can increase item value
	- [Tasting](#tasting) - another skill used to identify items

*Gold*{ .chip .chip-question }

- Use on unidentified liquids, reagent jars, and relics

- Related

## Combat

### Anatomy
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Use on enemies to determine their strength and dexterity
- Raise naturally while you fight with a weapon and heal with bandages
- Increase the amount of damage your weapons do
- Related
	- [Healing](#healing) - extra healing and bandage thresholds to cure poison and even resurrect players
	- [Psychology](#psychology) - evaluates mental stats instead of physical ones
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - also contributes to weapon damage

### Bludgeoning
!!! info "Classic name: Mace Fighting"

*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Hit and defend with hammers, clubs, staffs, wands, and maces
- Related
	- [Anatomy](#anatomy) - contributes to weapon damage
	- [Elementalism](#elementalism) - contributes to Staff/Wand weapon damage
	- [Mining](skills-secondary.md#mining) - provide damage bonus
	- [Magery](#magery) - contributes to Staff/Wand weapon damage
	- [Necromancy](#necromancy) - contributes to Staff/Wand weapon damage
	- [Ninjitsu](#ninjitsu) - contributes to weapon damage
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - contributes to weapon damage, unlocks weapon abilities

### Fencing
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Hit and defend with daggers and spears
- Related
	- [Anatomy](#anatomy) - contributes to weapon damage
	- [Ninjitsu](#ninjitsu) - contributes to weapon damage
	- [Poisoning](#poisoning) - many fencing weapons carry or apply poison
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - contributes to weapon damage, unlocks weapon abilities

### Fist Fighting
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Hit and defend with your hands (or pugilist gloves)
- Mages often learn this skill so their hands stay free to cast spells
- Used to determine power of *Mystic* class spells
- Related
	- [Focus](#focus) - *Mystic* class pre-requisite
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - *Mystic* class pre-requisite
	- [Spiritualism](#spiritualism) - enhances amounts restored
	- [Tailoring](skills-secondary.md#tailoring) - provide damage bonus to pugilist gloves

### Focus
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Dramatically improves Stamina regeneration
- Notably improves Mana regeneration
- Pre-requisite of the *Mystic* class
- Related
	- [Fist Fighting](#fist-fighting)
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - further improve mana regeneration, *Mystic* class pre-requisite

### Healing
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Use bandages to heal yourself during combat
- Activate this skill to Heal, cure poison, or stem bleeding
- Create cloth, cut it with scissors, and make your own bandages
- Pair with [Anatomy](#anatomy) for extra benefits
	- 60 skill in both will allow curing poison
	- 80 skill in both will allow you to resurrect others
- Dexterity lets you apply bandages quicker
- Pre-requisite for *Holy Man* class
- Related
	- [Alchemy](skills-secondary.md#alchemy) - alternative method to cure and heal
	- [Musicianship](#musicianship) - play health/mana regeneration songs
	- [Spiritualism](#spiritualism) - alternative self-healing using spiritual energy
	- [Veterinary](#veterinary) - alternative method to cure and heal creatures

### Marksmanship
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Increase chance to hit with ranged weapons as you use them
	- Bows, crossbows, harpoons
	- Throwing gloves
	- Wizard staves and some wands
- Seek out quivers to store arrows, bolts, and throwing weapons efficiently
- Related
	- [Anatomy](#anatomy) - contributes to weapon damage
	- [Bowcrafting](skills-secondary.md#bowcrafting) - provide damage bonus to bows and crossbows
	- [Ninjitsu](#ninjitsu) - contributes to weapon damage
	- [Seafaring](#seafaring) - provide damage bonus to harpoons
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - contributes to weapon damage, unlocks weapon abilities
	- [Tailoring](skills-secondary.md#tailoring) - provide damage bonus to throwing gloves

### Parrying
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Add the chance to negate all damage from a physical blow
- When wielding a shield, activating this skill will allow you to temporarly brace for impact
- Related
	- [Bushido](#bushido) - enables parrying with two-handed (melee) weapons
	- [Magic Resistance](#magic-resistance) - reduce duration of debuffs

### Swordsmanship
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Hit and defend with swords and axes
- Improves the more you use these weapons
- Used to determine power of *Jedi* and *Syth* spells
- Related
	- [Bushido](#bushido) - advantage with pole arms, axes, and other slashing weapons
	- [Lumberjacking](skills-secondary.md#lumberjacking) - provide damage bonus when using axes
	- [Parrying](#parrying) - defensive complement to any weapon skill
	- [Psychology](#psychology) - *Jedi* and *Syth* class pre-requisite
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - contributes to weapon damage

### Tactics
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Increase the amount of damage your weapons do
- Perform special attacks when paired with a weapon skill
	- Unlocks occur when both skills reach 70 and every 10 points after
	- [Bludgeoning](#bludgeoning)
	- [Fencing](#fencing)
	- [Fist Fighting](#fist-fighting) 
	- [Marksmanship](#marksmanship)
	- [Swordsmanship](#swordsmanship)
- Used to determine power of *Jedi* and *Syth* spells
- Related
	- [Anatomy](#anatomy) - also contributes to weapon damage
	- [Stealth](#stealth) - required for the shadow strike weapon ability

## Magic

### Bushido
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Purchase a Book of Bushido from a monk
- Add several defensive and offensive abilities
	- Use Lightning Strike attack to increase your chance to hit
- Add the ability to parry with a two-hand weapon
	- Reduce chances to parry when using a shield
- Increase weapon damage when using pole arms, axes, and other slashing weapons
- Related
	- [Parrying](#parrying) - samurai use this skill with a sword instead of a shield
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - contributes to weapon damage

### Elementalism
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }
*Pet*{ .chip .chip-bug }

!!! warning "Elementalism cannot be used with Magery or Necromancy"

- Utilize natural magics of a single element
- Purchase an Elementalist spellbook from an Elementalist
	- 5th+ sphere spells must be found as drops
- Consume mana and stamina per cast instead of reagents
	- Lower Reagent Cost attribute reduces the stamina cost
- Multiple tiers of summonable creatures
- Sanctuary spell provides access to the Lyceum
	- Masters may even do this in dungeons!
- Can use powerful wands
- Related
	- [Bludgeoning](#bludgeoning) - perform melee attacks and avoid attacks to prevent spell interruption
	- [Camping](#camping) - alternate way to access a shared space within dungeons
	- [Focus](#focus) - stamina and mana regeneration
	- [Magic Resistance](#magic-resistance) - reduce debuff, paralyze, and peacemaking durations
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - mana regeneration
	- [Parrying](#parrying) - avoid attacks to prevent spell interruption

### Knightship
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! info "Classic name: Chivalry"

!!! warning "Future plans will prevent casting when the player has negative karma"

- Augment physical-based fighter builds
	- [Bludgeoning](#bludgeoning)
	- [Fencing](#fencing)
	- [Fist Fighting](#fist-fighting) 
	- [Marksmanship](#marksmanship)
	- [Swordsmanship](#swordsmanship)
- Purchase a Book of Chivalry from a Knight
- Each cast consumes mana, stamina, and tithing points
	- Tithe gold at a shrine/ankh
- Pre-requisite to *Death Knight* class
- Related
	- [Necromancy](#necromancy) - complements *Death Knight* class
	- [Parrying](#parrying) - defensive complement for shielded combat

### Magery
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! warning "Magery cannot be used with Elementalism"

- Purchase a Magery Spellbook from a Mage
	- 5th+ circle spells must be found as drops
- Reagents are required to summon these magics
- Arguably required for *Arch Mage* class (Spell Research quest)
- Can use powerful wands
- Related
	- [Inscription](skills-secondary.md#inscription) - craft spellbooks and scrolls; bonus damage from cast spells
	- [Magic Resistance](#magic-resistance) - defensive counter to hostile Magery
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - regenerates the mana Magery consumes

### Magic Resistance
!!! info "Classic name: Resisting Spells"

*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Gain a natural resistance to damage
- Reduce the duration of detrimental spells and effects used against you
	- Curses
	- Paralyze
	- Peacemaking
- Related
	- [Bushido](#bushido) - *evasion* ability can negate all spell damage for a short time
	- [Parrying](#parrying) - when wielding a shield, activating Parrying can fully absorb damage

### Meditation
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Naturally increases mana regeneration
- Activate the skill to get up to double mana regeneration
	- If any of your armor prevents this, this skill provides zero mana regeneration
- Related
	- [Focus](#focus) - offers mana and stamina regeneration
	- [Spiritualism](#spiritualism) - restore health, stamina, and conditionally mana

### Necromancy
*Craft*{ .chip .chip-note }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! warning "Necromancy cannot be used with Elementalism"

!!! warning "Future plans will prevent casting when the player has positive karma"

- Study of dark magic and grotesque transformations
- Needs specific reagents, a necromancer spellbook, and the spells to put in it
- Arguably required for *Arch Mage* class (Spell Research quest)
- Can use powerful wands
- Related
	- [Forensics](skills-secondary.md#forensics) - pre-requisite to brew magical concoctions with a witch's cauldron
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - mana regeneration for sustained casting
	- [Poisoning](#poisoning) - provides poison resistance pierce at high skill levels
	- [Spiritualism](#spiritualism) - increases the potency of necromancy spells

### Ninjitsu
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Seek out a monk and get a Book of Ninjitsu to begin attacking from the shadows
- Pre-requisite for *Shinobi* class
- Enhances the ability to use any weapon or bare hands
- Further your ninja abilities by seeking the knowledge of the shinobi
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - conceal yourself before striking from the shadows
	- [Stealth](#stealth) - move while hidden

### Psychology
!!! info "Classic name: Evaluate Intelligence"

*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Actively using this on a target can help you determine their intelligence and remaining mana
- Used to determine power of class spells
	- *Jester* class
	- *Magery* skill
- Used to determine success rate of *Jedi* and *Syth* spells
- Related
	- [Begging](#begging) - pre-req for *Jester* class
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - related mana-focused skill
	- [Swordsmanship](#swordsmanship) - pre-req for *Jedi* and *Syth* class

### Spiritualism
!!! info "Classic name: Spirit Speak"

*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Increases the potency of necromancy spells and transformations
- Summon your own spiritual energy (mana) to heal wounds and restore some stamina
	- If a nearby corpse is consumed, mana is restored rather than deducted
- Cannot be used while poisoned
- Pre-requisite for *Holy Man* class
- Related
	- [Fist Fighting](#fist-fighting) - increases restoration amounts
	- [Healing](#healing) - alternative self-healing that cures poison and bleeding, *Holy Man* class pre-requisite
	- [Meditation](#meditation) - mana regeneration
	- [Necromancy](#necromancy) - extra power for necromancy spells

## Thieving

### Hiding
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Activate the skill to avoid being seen by monsters
- Nearby creatures may still reveal you
- Grandmasters and above have a chance to hide while combat
- Related
	- [Ninjitsu](#ninjitsu)
	- [Searching](#searching) - detect hidden players and objects
	- [Stealth](#stealth) - move while hidden

### Lockpicking
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Use lockpicks on locked boxes and chests to unlock them
- Required for Treasure Maps higher than level 2
	- Magic spells and other consumables will not work
- Related
	- [Cartography](#cartography) - decode treasure maps that lead to locked chests
	- [Remove Trap](#remove-trap) - chests are often both locked and trapped
	- [Tinkering](skills-secondary.md#tinkering) - craft your own lockpicks

### Poisoning
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! tip "Enable Classic Poisoning in HELP → Settings to poison weapons without Infectious Strike"

- Apply poison from a bottle to make certain weapons deal sickening blows
- Gain a chance to be immune to varying levels of poison
- Find poison from alchemists, or extract it from venom sacks into an empty bottle
- Infectious Strike lets you hit enemies with poisoned weapons
- Dump bottles of poison on the ground to infect those that walk over it
- The higher this skill, the more poison resistance you pierce
- Related
	- [Alchemy](skills-secondary.md#alchemy) - brew poison potions
	- [Fencing](#fencing) - many fencing weapons can apply poison
	- [Tactics](#tactics) - infectious strike weapon ability can apply poison
	- [Tasting](#tasting) - detect poison in food and drink

### Remove Trap
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Use on containers to disarm their traps
- Ignore or disable hidden traps when you walk near them
- Related
	- [Lockpicking](#lockpicking) - unlock chests after they are disarmed
	- [Searching](#searching) - detect hidden traps and doors
	- [Tinkering](skills-secondary.md#tinkering) - rarer metals increase trap kit damage

### Searching
!!! info "Classic name: Detect Hidden"

*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Reveal thief monsters that hide after stealing from you
- Use on yourself or the ground to detect nearby traps and treasure
- Passively detect traps when opening trapped containers or walking around
- Reveal secret doors
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - the skill Searching contests against
	- [Remove Trap](#remove-trap) - disarm traps after finding them
	- [Stealth](#stealth) - move while hidden to avoid detection
	- [Tracking](#tracking) - locate creatures over longer range by type

### Snooping
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Look in vendor packs for goods before stealing
- Access special coffers in towns
- Combine with [Stealing](#stealing) for access treasure pedestals in dungeons
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - stay hidden while snooping
	- [Stealing](#stealing) - steal items after snooping to see them

### Stealing
*Gold*{ .chip .chip-question }
*Loot*{ .chip .chip-success }

- Use on a vendor's backpack to steal an item at random
	- Target the exact item you wish to steal by [Snooping](#snooping) first
- Naturally rob the monsters you're fighting
- Combine with [Snooping](#snooping) for access treasure pedestals in dungeons
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - stay hidden while stealing
	- [Snooping](#snooping)
	- [Stealth](#stealth) - move silently to approach targets

### Stealth
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

!!! tip "To increase your skill at higher levels, you need to wear heavier equipment. Click and Talk to a Thief vendor can provide more explicit guidance."

- Walk around without being detected, including past enemies
- Safely get back to your corpse after you die
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - must be hidden first before using Stealth
	- [Tactics](#tactics)
	- [Tracking](#tracking) - tracking difficulty factors in a target's Stealth skill

### Tracking
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }

- Track your prey
	- Big game in the overworld
	- Quest monsters or NPCs
	- Thieves and Ninjas after they disappear
- Related
	- [Hiding](#hiding) - tracked targets use this to avoid detection
	- [Searching](#searching) - assists tracking checks against hidden targets
	- [Seafaring](#seafaring) - locate hidden sea creatures
	- [Stealth](#stealth) - tracked targets use this to avoid detection

## Barding

### Discordance
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! warning "This skill requires [Musicianship](#musicianship)"

- Play a song with a musical instrument to make an opponent less of a threat
	- Lowers their defenses and skills
- Improve strength and duration of bard songs
- Related
	- [Musicianship](#musicianship) - required for bardic skills to succeed
	- [Peacemaking](#peacemaking)
	- [Provocation](#provocation)

### Musicianship
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

- Primary base skill needed for bardic proclivities
- Purchase a Song Book from a Bard
	- Add song scrolls
	- Active gameplay with highly effective short duration buffs
- Related
	- [Discordance](#discordance) - lowers a target's defenses and abilities
	- [Peacemaking](#peacemaking) - pacifies hostile creatures
	- [Provocation](#provocation) - incites creatures to fight each other

### Peacemaking
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! warning "This skill requires [Musicianship](#musicianship)"

- Play music that makes even the most violent opponent stop attacking to listen
	- Targeting a specific enemy will pacify them for longer
	- Targeting yourself will attempt to briefly pacify all nearby targets
- Improve strength and duration of bard songs
- Related
	- [Begging](#begging) - can also pacify creatures without an instrument
	- [Discordance](#discordance)
	- [Musicianship](#musicianship) - required for bardic skills to succeed
	- [Provocation](#provocation)

### Provocation
*Combat*{ .chip .chip-warning }
*Magic*{ .chip .chip-example }

!!! warning "This skill requires [Musicianship](#musicianship)"

- Play musical instruments to cause havoc and make others fight each other
- Improve strength and duration of bard songs
- Related
	- [Discordance](#discordance)
	- [Musicianship](#musicianship) - required for bardic skills to succeed
	- [Peacemaking](#peacemaking)
