# Frequently Asked Questions

## Character
- What are the Character Caps ?
	- Default individual resistance cap is 70
	- Default skill cap is 1,000
	- Default stat cap is 250
	- Individual stat cap is 150 (hard cap)
	- A late game quest can provide additional skill and stat cap
- Do you have to consume Powerscrolls in order?
	- Yes, you must eat them in order. 105 -> 110 -> ... 125
- Can items overcap skills?
	- No, players must consume a powerscroll to exceed your current cap for a particular skill
		- There are actually a few *consumable items* or *items with Uses* that will allow overcapping but they are the outliers
- Why do my elemental resistances keep dropping to 60?
	- When Cursed, your maximum elemental resistance will be capped at 60
- Can I still gain skill if I'm at skill cap?
	- If your real/raw (not boosted) skill is not at it's skill cap (100 until powerscrolled), you can still gain skill
- Why am I not gaining skill?
	- If the task you're performing cannot fail, then you cannot improve your skill with that task

## What do I get from NPC Guilds?
- What do I get for joining an NPC guild?
	- Access to buy from and sell to the guildmaster
	- Increased skill gain chances for all guild skills
	- A membership ring with bonuses to guild skills
    - Guildmembers offer you more money for your goods
- Can I join more than one NPC guild?
	- No, you may only be in one NPC guild at a time
- What happens if I quit my guild?
	- Every time you quit, joining your next guild costs an additional 2,000 gold
	- This increase stacks with no limit, so choose carefully

!!! tip "Your guild ring does *not* need to be equipped for the acceleration to apply"

## What? There's more facets than just Sosaria?
- There are a total of `6` facets
- To make it past Sosaria, it's recommended to get a Boat and explore every island

## I'm bored. Now what do I do?
- The server is rich with Lore that often leads to personal quests
	- If you haven't already, head to Dungeon Clues for some... clues
	- Lore books are memorized the first time you read them. You can access your `Library` by using `Help -> Gumps` 

!!! tip "*Librian Tools* can be used on Bookshelves to help you find Lore books and more"

- Use `[feats` to seek out new towns or dungeons on the Lands you've discovered
- Create a new character
	- Consider selecting [Temptations](temptations.md)
	- Use a non-standard skill or template
	- Begin [The Avatar's Ascent](avatars-ascent.md)
	- Begin with a Monster race and build a template around their perks
- Raise your [Secondary Skills](../guide/skills-secondary.md), they don't count against your skill cap
	- Speak to crafting vendors and discover how to open a Shoppe
- Engage in homeownership
	- Visit Architects and peruse their novel decorations
	- Use the `Customize` option from your house sign
	- Add custom decorations using *Lawn tools* or *Remodeling tools*
	- Raise plants or bees
	- Seek out the city of Furance to learn the art stonemasonry or glassblowing
- Hop on a boat to find and complete [Seafaring](../guide/skills-primary.md#seafaring) content

!!! tip "Seafaring is a Primary skill, but it is not required to partake in any of the content"
